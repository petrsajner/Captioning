Zorvak style, a wide night shot shows a herd of pale deer beneath a large, bare tree. The prominent, leafless tree dominates the left foreground, its thick, twisting branches curving toward the center. Deer stand and sit on the dark ground, some facing forward while others turn their heads. Patches of bright white snow contrast with the dark earth. In the background, a dark forest line stretches across the horizon, dotted with small, light-colored structures. The composition places the tree and deer in the lower half, leaving the upper half filled by a dark blue sky scattered with white stars.
