Zorvak style, a calm river flows through a natural landscape featuring several white cranes: one stands among reeds, another perches nearby, a third flies low, and a fourth takes off from the bank. Small wooden boats float on the water, one carrying yellow grain. The opposite shore displays slender trees, thatched-roof houses, and scattered deciduous trees. A distant mountain range rises against a sky transitioning from deep blue to a warm reddish-orange glow near the horizon. Vertical columns of Japanese text appear in the upper right and lower left corners.
