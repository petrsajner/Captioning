Zorvak style, a medium eye-level shot captures a tranquil riverside scene at sunrise or sunset, with a soft pink and orange sky. White herons are active, with one flying, another standing in the water, and two foraging in tall reeds. Small boats, some carrying harvested reeds, dot the calm river.
